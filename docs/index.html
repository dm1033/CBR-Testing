The DCP we sell

Methodology

Four pads. One certificate.

01 · Build the platform

Min 300 mm, preferably 400 mm MOT Type 1, layers ≤ 150 mm. Photos at each outrigger.

02 · Probe four pads

8 kg hammer, 575 mm drop. Count blows every 100 mm to 600 mm at all four outrigger centres.

03 · Convert to CBR

GroundCheck uses ASTM D6951 / USACE. Screening q_all ≈ 10 × CBR (kPa). Weak layers are flagged.

04 · Issue the certificate

All four pass + dry + records complete = Route A pads. Anything else = Route B 1.5 × 1.5 m mats.

Screening CBR from DCP (ASTM D6951 / USACE). Not a substitute for BS 1377-9 plate bearing. Subject to stock and a competent person on site.
